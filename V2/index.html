FAQ

More questions? Answers.

Everything you need to know before the call.

What is Openstage?

Openstage is the Fan Relationship Platform that helps artists grow by building stronger relationships with their fans. One place to build and grow your fan ecosystem: give fans somewhere to join and go deeper, know who your fans are and how much they care, and create better fan experiences that deepen fandom and drive Fan-Led Growth.

What tools can Openstage replace?

Your website, link-in-bio, mailing list, SMS, pre-save and membership tools, connected around one fan relationship instead of scattered across six logins.

How is Openstage different from Mailchimp, Klaviyo or Laylo?

Those tools optimize a moment: a send, a drop, an on-sale. Openstage optimizes the relationship, so every moment builds on the last.

Can I bring my existing fan data into Openstage?

Yes. Bring in existing lists and connect streaming, ticketing, merch and social so every fan you already have starts with a complete profile.

Who owns my fan data?

You do. Openstage is open by design: your fan relationships, identity and access work across your ecosystem and go wherever you do.

Is Openstage right for my team?

Openstage is built for artists and their teams with a fanbase worth building on. Book a demo and we'll walk through how it fits your roster.
