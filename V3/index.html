Problem

Growth is broken.

The industry spent the past decade optimizing for attention.
Artists have learned to grow from the outside in: continually chasing attention, sending fans into platforms they don't control, and hoping some of it turns into lasting fandom.

Expensive.

You have to keep paying, posting and competing to reach people again.

Unreliable.

What works today can disappear tomorrow because the platform controls the relationship.

Doesn't compound.

You don't know who the fan is, what they did next, or whether it turned into anything.

FAQ

More questions?

Here's what most artists ask first.

What is Openstage?

Openstage is the Fan Relationship Platform that helps artists grow by building stronger relationships with their fans. Purpose-built around the belief that growth is defined not just by how many fans an artist has, but how much they care, Openstage helps artists turn deeper fandom into Fan-Led Growth. Combining artist-owned fan experiences with the richest fan data in music, Openstage powers artist-fan relationships for over 30 million fans worldwide.

How is Openstage different from direct-to-fan and CRM tools?

Those tools are built for customers and optimize the moment: a send, a drop, an on-sale. Openstage is built for fans and optimizes the artist-fan relationship. You still get everything they do, but every interaction connects back to who your fans are and how much they care, so every moment builds fandom, not just reach.

What tools can Openstage replace?

Your website, link-in-bio, mailing list, SMS, pre-save, and membership tools. Most artists are paying for five or six things that Openstage does in one place, connected.

How easy is it to switch?

Openstage is built to be self-serve, with a full knowledge base, video tutorials and step-by-step guides to get you started. And our onboarding and support teams are with you from day one if you need them, covering migration, integrations and setup every step of the way.

Do I need to be a data expert to use Openstage?

No. Openstage makes the richest fan data in music easy to understand and act on. The dashboard gives you a clear view of your fanbase, and Openstage MCP connects that data to Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini so you can ask questions in plain English, find opportunities, shape strategy and know what to do next. No exports, no spreadsheets, no learning curve.

Can I bring my existing fan data into Openstage?

Yes. We support imports from most major platforms and our team will help you consolidate what you already have into one clean fanbase.

Who owns my fan data?

You do. Always. Openstage never sells or shares your fan data with third parties. What you build on Openstage stays yours.

What does Openstage cost?

Openstage comes in two plans: Build, for artists growing their fanbase, and Legendary, for artists operating at scale. See the pricing page for a full breakdown, or book a demo and we'll recommend the right plan for your needs.
